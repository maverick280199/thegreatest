## 2. Chosen niche
**"Ghar, Parivaar aur Zameen ka Kanoon"** — family + property law for Hindi-belt families
(marriage, divorce, NRI marriages, maintenance, inheritance, daughters' rights, wills, registry, land disputes).
Why: the two biggest-demand lanes; money + emotion in every topic; a steady flow of SC/HC rulings; property
is currently owned by finance creators, not advocates, so an advocate's authority is a real edge; and rumour
reels ("naya kanoon aa gaya") prove there's an audience that needs a correct voice. Reel #2 (NRI divorce) is
already squarely in it.

## 3. Formats that are breaking out (use all five, rotate)
1. **Viral clip → "Kanoon kya kehta hai"** (strongest across niches: 114x, 86x, 30x, 25x). 2-4 s of a trending
   family/property clip on top, you below, then the law. Use only a few seconds, credit the source, criticism/
   review purpose (Copyright Act s.52); never court footage. Speed matters: post within 24-48 h of the trend.
2. **Judgment in 60 sec** — our paper-card style with your cutout (Reel #2). Story → twist → one-line law.
3. **Myth-buster** — "Ye viral 'naya kanoon' FAKE hai" (the 12.2M reel was misinformation). Show the viral
   claim, stamp FAKE, give the real section. Uniquely credible from an advocate.
4. **Numbered list** — "Zameen khareedne se pehle ye 5 documents" (65x for helpline list; 47x/242x for registry lists).
5. **Case story with twist** — the NRI script structure (verdict first, story, "Supreme Court ka twist", quotable line).

## 4. Hook rules seen in every winner
- First frame has Hindi text that states a loss or a surprise: "Registry se aap maalik nahi", "Pehli shaadi abhi zinda hai".
- Top-half visual (clip / document / bold graphic), creator bottom half. Static talking head alone underperforms.
- Specific number or name in the first line (60 din, 7 saal, ₹98,000, Supreme Court).
- End with a send ask aimed at a person ("NRI dost ko bhejo", "ghar ke bade ko dikhao") — shares drive reach.

## 5. Compliance guardrails (BCI framework, 17 July 2026 + Rule 36 + Bar Council of Delhi notice Aug 2025)
- No court / chamber / court-corridor footage. No "DM for consultation", phone numbers, fees, "best lawyer",
  client testimonials or case wins — several big accounts do this; it is exactly what BCI is targeting.
- Educational only; disclaimer on every post; promote the law, not the lawyer.

## 6. Cadence for "organic but fast"
- 4 reels/week: Mon judgment · Wed evergreen family/property · Fri myth-buster or list · +1 trend-reaction
  whenever a family/property clip goes viral (within 48 h).
- 1 carousel/week (saves) repurposing the best reel.
- 20 min/day commenting (types 1-2 from /ig-comment) on property/finance creators and family-law pages —
  their audiences are ours. No pitching.
- Measure weekly: outlier multiple vs our own median, sends per reach, non-follower reach. Double down on
  whichever format wins two weeks running; drop the bottom one.
- Style refresh monthly: re-run the outlier search, steal the format (never the content), update this file.
