# Voice — Pundir & Associates (@pundirandassociates)

Who speaks: an Indian advocate's chambers explaining the law to ordinary citizens.
Audience: Indian public, 20-50, not lawyers. Hindi-English bilingual; write in simple
English a Class 10 student understands, with section numbers on screen, not jargon in speech.

Tone: calm, exact, a little sharp. A senior counsel explaining to a worried client,
not a news anchor. Short sentences. One idea per reel.

Always:
- Cite the actual provision (Article / Section / Act) or the judgment (name + year).
- Use the new criminal laws (BNS / BNSS / BSA, in force 1 July 2024) and mention the
  old CrPC/IPC number in brackets when people still know it by that.
- End the caption with: "For general awareness only. Not legal advice."

Never (Bar Council of India Rules, Rule 36):
- No solicitation: no "DM us", "call us", "book a consultation", "hire us", no phone numbers.
- No claims of results, success rates, case wins, client testimonials.
- No "best / top / expert / No.1 lawyer".
- No fee mentions, no offers.
- No invented statistics. If a number isn't in a statute or judgment, it doesn't go in.

Look: minimalist. Off-white/ivory background, deep navy + muted gold accent, one flat
line-art avatar (the "Counsel" character: an advocate in a black coat and white bands),
flat icons, lots of whitespace, big type for the section number.
