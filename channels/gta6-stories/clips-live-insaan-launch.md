## Cutting notes

- Layout: facecam on top (his reaction), gameplay below. The reaction is what makes the clip transformative and watchable.
- Keep at most 20 seconds of his footage per Short. The narration carries the rest, over a freeze-frame or a zoom.
- Bleep or mute the swearing in the Hindi versions; YouTube's family-friendly settings matter for reach in India.
- Credit on screen: `Clip: Live Insaan` in the bottom corner, plus a link in the description.
