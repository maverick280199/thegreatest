## Where each fact comes from (the game's own tips)

- **Heat stays in the area:** "Heat will eventually die down. Use the map to see areas where you still have heat. Cops are actively looking for you in this area." (game tip, Live Insaan stream 2:47:24)
- **Hat and glasses:** "Adjust your hat and glasses to lower recognition." then "Cops cannot identify you now." (game tips, Live Insaan stream 2:45:01, 2:46:50)
- **Vehicle swap:** in Part 1 the radio updated the vehicle ("They are now driving a pickup") because the swap was seen. Lose sight first. (Part 1 footage; TGM free roam 24:04)
- **Cop scanner:** "Now you'll be able to see when and where the cops are being dispatched." and "Cop scanner installed. You can now listen in on cop radio frequencies." (game dialogue and tip, Shirrako 49:23 / Live Insaan 2:27:59)
