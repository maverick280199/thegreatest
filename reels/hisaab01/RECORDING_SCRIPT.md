1.  ₹1 lakh ka cheque bounce hua? Paisa doobega nahi. Kanoon ke hisaab se ₹2 lakh tak mil sakte hain.
2.  Kaise? Hisaab samjhiye. Kanoon Ka Hisaab — episode ek.
3.  Pehle ghadi. Bank se cheque wapas aaya, return memo mila.
4.  30 din ke andar legal notice bhejo.
5.  Saamne wale ke paas paisa lautane ke 15 din.
6.  Nahi lautaya? Agle ek mahine mein complaint file karo. Section 138, NI Act.
7.  Yaad rakho: 30, 15, 30. Deadline chooki, toh case kamzor.
8.  Ab paisa. Case chalte-chalte hi court 20 percent tak interim compensation dila sakta hai. Yaani ₹20,000 tak. Section 143A.
9.  Conviction hua? Fine, cheque ka do guna tak. ₹1 lakh par ₹2 lakh tak. Aur court ye rakam aapko compensation mein dila sakta hai.
10. Saath mein do saal tak ki jail bhi ho sakti hai.
11. Accused appeal mein gaya? Pehle fine ya compensation ka kam se kam 20 percent jama karna padega. Section 148.
12. Ab twist. Agar accused case jeet gaya, toh wo interim 20 percent aapko byaaj ke saath lautana padega.
13. Isliye case tabhi karo, jab saboot pakka ho ki paisa sach mein bakaaya tha.
14. Kanoon ka hisaab saaf hai: deadline pakdo, saboot rakho.
15. Kisi ka cheque aapke paas pada hai? Us dost ko ye reel bhejo. Agla hisaab: property mein kisko kitna hissa.

Legal check (Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881):
- s.138 + provisos (b),(c): notice within 30 days of the bank's return memo; drawer has 15 days to pay.
- s.142(1)(b): complaint within one month of the cause of action (delay condonable for sufficient cause) -> "kamzor", not "khatam".
- s.138: imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine up to twice the cheque amount, or both. Compensation out of the fine: BNSS s.395 (old CrPC 357).
- s.143A: interim compensation up to 20% of cheque amount; s.143A(4): refund with interest if acquitted.
- s.148: appellate court may order deposit of at least 20% of fine/compensation, in addition to s.143A.
- Legally enforceable debt is required (s.138 Explanation) -> line 13.
Sources: Bar & Bench (ss.138 & 143A), devgan.in (s.148), Uttarakhand judicial academy note on interim compensation, Income Tax Dept/indiacode text references.
