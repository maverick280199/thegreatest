₹1 lakh ka cheque bounce hua? Kanoon ke hisaab se ₹2 lakh tak mil sakte hain. Poora hisaab 👇

Kanoon Ka Hisaab · Episode 1

⏰ Deadline: 30 · 15 · 30
1. Bank ka return memo milne ke 30 din ke andar legal notice.
2. Saamne wale ko paisa lautane ke 15 din.
3. Nahi lautaya? Agle 1 mahine mein complaint. (Section 138, NI Act)

💰 Paisa
- Case ke beech: interim compensation, cheque ka 20% tak (Section 143A)
- Conviction par: fine cheque ka 2 guna tak, jo court compensation mein dila sakta hai; 2 saal tak jail bhi
- Appeal se pehle: fine/compensation ka kam se kam 20% jama (Section 148)

🔄 Twist: accused acquit hua toh interim 20% byaaj ke saath lautana padega (Section 143A(4)). Isliye case tabhi, jab saboot pakka ho.

Kisi ka cheque aapke paas pada hai? Us dost ko ye reel bhejo.
Agla hisaab: property mein kisko kitna hissa?
