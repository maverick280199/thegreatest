Arrest ke waqt police ko yeh 4 cheezein karni hi padti hain.

1. Arrest ka kaaran batana (BNSS S.47(1))
2. Bailable offence ho toh bail ka haq batana (BNSS S.47(2))
3. Aapke rishtedaar ya dost ko suchna dena (BNSS S.48)
4. 24 ghante ke andar Magistrate ke saamne pesh karna (BNSS S.58)

Aur Article 22(1) aapko apni pasand ke vakeel se salah ka haq deta hai.
