# Reel 02 — recording script v2 (Hinglish, ~55-60 sec)

Delivery: first two lines fast and punchy, like breaking news. Story part slower, like telling a client
a real case. Pause a full beat before "Supreme Court ka twist". Last line warm, like advice to a friend.
Record in a quiet room, phone 15-20 cm from your mouth. Any format works (voice note, .m4a, .mp3, .wav).

1.  UK mein divorce liya. Doosri shaadi ki. Aur Supreme Court ne kaha — pehli shaadi abhi bhi zinda hai.
2.  Ab us pati par bigamy ka case chalega. Saat saal tak ki saza wala.

8.  Teen November 2026. Supreme Court ka twist.
9.  Hindu Marriage Act mein "irretrievable breakdown" koi ground hi nahi hai.
10. Toh Indian law ke khilaaf diya gaya foreign divorce, India mein nahi chalega. Section 13 CPC.
11. Kaagaz par divorce. Kanoon ki nazar mein — pehli shaadi kayam.
12. Passport badalne se kanoon nahi badalta.
13. Haan, parents aur doosri patni ke khilaaf case khatam hua. Par pati ka case chalega.
14. Seedhi baat: shaadi Indian law se hui hai, toh divorce bhi Indian law ke hisaab se valid hona chahiye.
15. Kisi NRI dost ki shaadi ya divorce ki baat chal rahi hai? Ye reel usse abhi bhejo.
