# Reel 02 — recording script (Hinglish, ~50 sec)

Read naturally, like you're explaining to a client. Leave a short breath (½ second) at every line break.
Record in a quiet room, phone 15-20 cm from your mouth. Any format works (voice note, .m4a, .mp3, .wav).

1. Videsh mein divorce le liya, aur doosri shaadi bhi kar li?
2. Supreme Court ne kaha — case chalega.
3. Teen November 2026 ka faisla hai.
4. Shaadi 2003 mein India mein, Hindu reeti-rivaaz se hui.
5. Pati ne 2014 mein UK court se divorce le liya, irretrievable breakdown ke ground par, aur doosri shaadi kar li.
6. Patni ne us UK court ka jurisdiction maana hi nahi.
7. Supreme Court ne kaha — Hindu Marriage Act mein ye ground hai hi nahi.
8. Isliye ye foreign decree Section 13 CPC par fail, aur pehli shaadi ab bhi zinda hai.
9. British citizenship lene se bhi bachaav nahi.
10. Pati par bigamy ka case chalega.
11. Lesson simple hai.
12. Shaadi Indian law se hui hai, toh divorce bhi Indian law ke hisaab se valid hona chahiye.
13. Ye reel save karo, aur NRI shaadi wale har dost ko bhejo.
