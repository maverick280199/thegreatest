Videsh mein divorce le liya, India wali shaadi khatam? Supreme Court ne kaha: nahi.

Supreme Court, 3 November 2026 (Justice Prashant Kumar Mishra and Justice Shree Chandrashekhar):

1. Shaadi 2003 mein India mein, Hindu Marriage Act ke under hui.
2. Pati ne 2014 mein UK court se "irretrievable breakdown" ke ground par divorce liya aur doosri shaadi kar li.
3. Patni ne UK court ka jurisdiction maana hi nahi.
4. Ye ground Hindu Marriage Act mein hai hi nahi, isliye foreign decree Section 13 CPC par fail. Pehli shaadi kayam.
5. British citizenship se bhi bachaav nahi. Pati par bigamy (IPC 494, ab BNS 82) ka case chalega.

Indian law se hui shaadi ka divorce bhi Indian law ke hisaab se valid hona chahiye.

Save karein. NRI shaadi wale kisi bhi dost ko share karein.
