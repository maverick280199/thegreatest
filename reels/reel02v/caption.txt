UK mein divorce liya, doosri shaadi ki. Supreme Court: pehli shaadi abhi bhi zinda hai.

Supreme Court, 3 November 2026 (Justice Prashant Kumar Mishra and Justice Shree Chandrashekhar):

1. Shaadi 2003 mein India mein, Hindu reeti-rivaaz se.
2. Pati ne 2014 mein UK court se "irretrievable breakdown" ke ground par divorce liya, doosri shaadi ki aur British citizen ban gaya.
3. Patni ne UK court ka jurisdiction kabhi maana hi nahi.
4. Hindu Marriage Act mein ye ground hai hi nahi. Isliye foreign decree Section 13 CPC par fail, pehli shaadi kayam.
5. Parents aur doosri patni ke khilaaf case khatam, lekin pati par bigamy (IPC 494, ab BNS 82) ka case chalega.

Passport badalne se kanoon nahi badalta.
