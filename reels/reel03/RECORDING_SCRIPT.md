# Reel 03 — "Registry ho gayi, phir bhi zameen aapki nahi" (Hinglish, ~55 sec)
Slot: Wed 18 Nov 2026, 7:30 pm IST · Format: myth + list · Look: paper cards + your uniform cutout

Delivery: line 1 like a warning to a friend who just bought a plot. Lines 3-8 like telling a real case.
Pause before "Supreme Court ka faisla". The 3-point checklist slow and clear.
Record as a VIDEO (chest-up, plain light wall, face a window) if you want the moving cutout,
or as a voice note for the photo cutout. Retakes are fine: just pause and repeat the line.

1.  Registry ho gayi? Badhai ho. Lekin zameen phir bhi aapki nahi ho sakti.
2.  Ye main nahi keh raha — Supreme Court keh raha hai.
3.  May 2025. Telangana mein 53 acre zameen ka case.
4.  Kharidaron ke paas registered sale deed thi. Kaagaz poore.
5.  Par bechne wale ka apna haq ek 1982 ke agreement par tika tha — jo kabhi register hi nahi hua.
6.  Supreme Court ka faisla: registry ek procedure hai. Wo galat title ko sahi nahi bana sakti.
7.  Malikana haq wahi de sakta hai, jo khud maalik ho.
8.  Bechne wale ke paas haq nahi, toh aapki registry bhi kamzor.
9.  Toh registry se pehle ye teen cheezein zaroor dekho.
10. Ek — title chain. Zameen kis-kis ke haath se aayi, har kaagaz.
11. Do — encumbrance certificate. Zameen par koi loan ya case toh nahi.
12. Teen — revenue record mein mutation, bechne wale ke naam par.
13. Registry aakhri step hai, pehla nahi.
14. Ghar ya plot lene wale kisi bhi dost ko ye reel bhejo.

Legal check — Mahnoor Fatima Imran & Ors. v. Visweswara Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. (SC, 7 May 2025):
- ~53-acre Telangana land; title traced to an unregistered 1982 agreement of sale.
- Held: registration records a transaction, it does not cure a defective title; title must flow from a
  person who has it; a registered deed built on an unregistered/void foundation confers no valid title.
- TPA s.54: sale of immovable property (₹100+) only by registered instrument; an agreement to sell
  creates no interest. Checklist items (title chain, EC, mutation) are standard due diligence, not from
  the judgment — script says "zaroor dekho", not "Supreme Court ne kaha".
Sources: Bar & Bench, Nyaaya (18 Jul 2025), LiveLaw, SC Observer, Business Standard.
