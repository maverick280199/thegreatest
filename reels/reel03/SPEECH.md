# Reel 03 — Descriptive speech: "Registry ho gayi, phir bhi zameen aapki nahi" (~2 min)
Format: free-flowing speech + infographics on highlight words. Speak naturally; paraphrasing is fine.
Keep the FIRST paragraph close to word-for-word (it is the hook).

## Speech

Registry ho gayi, stamp lag gaya, kaagaz haath mein aa gaye — aur aapko lagta hai ki ab zameen aapki hai. Lekin main aapko bata doon, Supreme Court ne saaf keh diya hai: sirf registry se aap maalik nahi bante.

Ek case suniye. May 2025 mein Supreme Court ke saamne Telangana ki lagbhag 53 acre zameen ka maamla aaya. Kharidaron ke paas registered sale deeds thi, sab kuch sarkari daftar mein darj tha. Unhe laga, kaam ho gaya. Par jab court ne zameen ki kahani peeche tak khodi, toh pata chala ki bechne wale ka apna haq ek 1982 ke agreement par tika tha — aur wo agreement kabhi register hi nahi hua tha. Yaani jis buniyaad par poori imaarat khadi thi, wo buniyaad hi kachchi thi.

Supreme Court ne kaha: registration ek procedure hai. Wo sirf ek transaction ko record karta hai. Agar bechne wale ke paas khud haq hi nahi tha, toh registry us kami ko theek nahi kar sakti. Malikana haq hamesha usi se aata hai jo khud asli maalik ho. Aap kisi ko wo cheez nahi bech sakte jo aapki hai hi nahi.

Ab sawaal ye hai ki aap apne aap ko kaise bachayein? Teen cheezein, registry se pehle, zaroor dekhiye.

Pehli — title chain. Zameen pichhle kai saalon mein kis-kis ke haath se guzri, har sale deed, har varasat, har kaagaz dekhiye. Beech mein ek bhi kadi kamzor hui, toh poori chain kamzor.

Doosri — encumbrance certificate. Isse pata chalta hai ki zameen par koi loan, girvi ya case toh nahi chal raha.

Teesri — revenue record aur mutation. Khatauni ya jamabandi mein zameen bechne wale ke naam par darj honi chahiye, aur registry ke baad aapka naam bhi chadhna chahiye.

Aur ek baat yaad rakhiye — agreement to sell se malikana haq nahi milta. Haq tabhi transfer hota hai jab registered sale deed bane, aur wo bhi tab, jab bechne wale ka apna haq saaf ho.

Toh registry aakhri step hai, pehla nahi. Pehle kaagaz jaanchiye, phir paisa dijiye.

Agar aapke ghar mein koi plot ya flat lene ki soch raha hai, ye video unhe zaroor bhejiye. Ek baar ki jaanch, zindagi bhar ki tension bacha sakti hai.

## Legal check
- Mahnoor Fatima Imran & Ors. v. Visweswara Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. (SC, 7 May 2025): registration records a
  transaction and does not cure defective title; title must flow from one who has it; title traced to an
  unregistered 1982 agreement of sale failed. (Bar & Bench, Nyaaya, LiveLaw, SC Observer, Business Standard.)
- Transfer of Property Act s.54: sale of immovable property (₹100+) only by registered instrument; an agreement
  to sell creates no interest in the property.
- The 3-point checklist is standard due diligence (advice), not a holding of the judgment — spoken as advice.
- Names of revenue records vary by state (khatauni UP, jamabandi Punjab/Haryana/Rajasthan, 7/12 Maharashtra).
